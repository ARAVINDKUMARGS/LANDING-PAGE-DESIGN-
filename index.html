Amateur Astronomy Journal

Log the sky, before it moves on.

Aphelion is a field log for people who point a scope or a pair of binoculars at the dark and want to remember what they saw. Track sessions, conditions, and sightings — searchable by object, date, or sky position.

What goes in the log

Three habits worth keeping, built into the app instead of a paper notebook you'll lose by August.

SESSIONtimestamped

Every session, dated and placed

Open a session and Aphelion records the time, your location, and pulls current seeing and transparency conditions automatically. You just note what you pointed at and how it looked.

OBJECTScross-referenced

Sightings linked to a catalog

Log a sighting against Messier, NGC, or a custom object, and Aphelion keeps a running history of every time you've observed it — magnitude estimates, sketches, and notes included.

SEARCHby sky or date

Find a night by what was in it

Search your log by object, constellation, equipment, or conditions. "Every clear night I looked at Saturn" is a query, not a memory you have to trust.
